What happens when you call

From ring to knock on the door, in five steps. You hear the full price before anyone gets in the van.

  1. We answer

    Every call picked up in three rings, day or night. Missed? You get a text straight back.

  2. You show us

    Tell us what broke and send a photo. If it's dangerous, we tell you what to do first.

  3. You get one price

    Your card is held, not charged. You get a live arrival time by text.

  5. Safe, then fixed

    We make it safe tonight and quote the permanent repair before we leave.

Questions

How do you know you'll make it in 60 minutes?

We only promise addresses inside our core area, set by postcode and real drive times. Outside it, we'll still come the same day without the guarantee.

When does the clock start?

When your booking is confirmed on the phone. You get a text with the promised time.

What if you can't get in when you arrive?

If no one's there or the job's cancelled after we set off, the call-out is still charged. We tell you this before we leave.

Can I cancel after booking?

Emergency repairs you ask us to do straight away aren't covered by the usual 14-day cancellation period. You agree to this by text when you book.

Do you work 24 hours?

Yes, every day of the year, including nights and bank holidays.
